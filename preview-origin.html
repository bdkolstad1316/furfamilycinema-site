Pets Are Family. Period.

It started with a broken heart. While working as a veterinary assistant, the founder watched sweet souls spend long days in kennels — waiting, watching, hoping for a little affection.

That’s when the dream began: a place where pets could truly feel at home. Not just cared for, but loved. A space to move, play, rest, and connect.

Today Fur Family Cinema is more than a pet care facility — it’s a community built on compassion, safety, and joy. We’ve supported rescues whenever we can, because giving back is part of who we are.

When a beloved local theater found its next chapter, it became the perfect stage for something new. The Lewiston Tribune has followed the journey — from opening day to giving pets the star treatment they deserve. We were also featured in Big Country News Connection’s Sunday Small Business Spotlight.
